10 viral hook formulas that still work in 2026

Formulas are scaffolding. If you paste them without a specific object — a Costco chicken, a $4 thrift jacket, a wet rice cooker — they sound like every other “viral hooks” carousel.

How to practice

  1. Pick one object in your room.
  2. Write all ten formulas about that object in ten minutes.
  3. Circle the two you would say to a coworker, not to an algorithm.
  4. Film only those two. Do not film the clever one you would never say.

FAQ

What is a hook formula?

A reusable first-line pattern. You fill it with your object, not a celebrity name.

Which formula is best for TikTok?

Question and mistake still travel. Test two formulas on the same video idea.

Can I mix formulas?

Yes. Many strong opens are a mistake plus a number. Keep the spoken line under 12 words.
