Facebook is not TikTok with a blue icon

The median Facebook Reels viewer is older, often watching on Wi-Fi at the kitchen table. Jargon dies. Specific household objects live. This Facebook Reels hook generator leans toward plain US English.

CTAs that work here: share to a Page, comment a number, send to a sibling. “Link in bio” is weaker than on Instagram.

Should I post the same Reel to Facebook and Instagram?

You can cross-post the video. Rewrite the hook and hashtags. This page exists so that rewrite takes one minute.

Do hashtags matter on Facebook Reels?

Less than on TikTok. A clear first line and a shareable caption matter more.
