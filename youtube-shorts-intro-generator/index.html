Hooks are short on purpose. If a line is longer than 8–12 words, cut it before you film.

Shorts are not vertical YouTube

People open Shorts with the sound off more often than they admit. Your intro has to work as on-screen text first, voice second. That is why this YouTube Shorts intro generator always returns a short overlay line.

Pair it with a hard visual: hands already doing the thing, not a face saying hello. Then stack a spoken line that matches the overlay so accessibility and retention both win.

Intro patterns that still work

  • Number + object: “3 resume lines US hiring managers skip.”
  • Negative: “Do not start your Short with your name.”
  • Mistake: “This is why your rice looks wet on camera.”
  • Loop: last frame should make the first frame make sense again.

FAQ

How long should a YouTube Shorts intro be?

The first second. Overlay text should be readable in a glance — under 8 words if you can.

Does YouTube rank Shorts on the first line?

Retention and audience signals matter more than a keyword in the spoken intro. Still, a clear first line helps humans stay.

Can I use these intros on long videos?

You can. Long videos still need a cold open. Just do not stuff the same 8-word line into a 12-minute intro.
