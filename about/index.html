About HookForge

HookForge is a small web tool. It writes first lines, on-screen text, captions, and hashtags for short-form video. It does not run ads inside the generator today. It does not sell your ideas.

We built it because “creator education” sites hide the useful part behind an email wall. The useful part is a sentence you can say in one breath.

Who it is for

US-based TikTok, Shorts, Reels, and Facebook Reels makers who already have a niche and need a draft opener. Students, side hustlers, small shops, hobby cooks — not agencies buying 10,000 spam blogs.

Who it is not for

Medical, legal, or get-rich advice. We will not generate supplement claims or crypto pitches on purpose. If a line sounds like a guarantee, delete it.

How ranking is supposed to work

On-page: one intent per URL, real H1, FAQs, internal links, schema. Technical: static files, canonicals, sitemap, robots, RSS, llms.txt, hreflang. Off-page: embeddable widget, swipe file people can cite, RSS for news readers. No fake reviews.

Contact: contact page. Privacy: privacy.
