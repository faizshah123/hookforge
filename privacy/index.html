Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-03.

HookForge is a static site plus client-side JavaScript. Generating hooks does not require an account.

What we collect

  • Standard web logs on whatever host serves the files (IP, user agent, referrer). We do not use those to sell ads today.
  • Optional local history in your browser (localStorage key hf-hist) so shuffle can remember recent niches. You can clear site data anytime.
  • If you email us, we keep the message long enough to reply.

What we do not collect

  • TikTok, Google, Meta, or CapCut passwords. We never ask.
  • Payment cards. The tool is free.
  • Health or financial account data. Do not paste it into the idea box.

Cookies

No tracking pixels ship with this version. If a future host injects analytics, this page will name it.

Children

The site is written for adults and working teens who already post publicly. It is not directed at children under 13.
