How to write a hook in under 10 seconds

Set a timer. You get ten seconds of thinking, not ten seconds of filming. The drill:

  1. Name one object in the frame (2 seconds).
  2. Pick one formula: question, mistake, or number (2 seconds).
  3. Say a line under 12 words out loud (6 seconds).

If you cannot say it, you cannot film it. Write it down. If the timer rings on mush, hit the hook generator with that object as the idea and steal a cleaner version.

Examples

  • Object: cracked screen. Line: “Do not film this crack until the overlay is on.”
  • Object: meal-prep containers. Line: “Sunday containers that are not aesthetic. They work.”
  • Object: thrift jacket. Line: “$4 jacket. Work outfit. No haul music.”
