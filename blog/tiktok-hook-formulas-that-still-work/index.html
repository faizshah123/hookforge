TikTok hook formulas that still work in 2026

Trend audio changes weekly. First-line jobs do not. If a stranger cannot tell what the video is about before the beat drops, you do not have a hook. You have a shrug.

This piece maps the same ten patterns we ship in the TikTok hook generator. Steal the pattern. Replace the object. Do not steal someone else’s story.

1. Question

Best when the viewer is already guilty. “Wait, you season chicken in the pan?” works because millions of US kitchens do. “Are you ready to level up?” works for nobody.

2. Contrarian

Pick a tip your niche repeats. Kill it in one clause. Then show the replacement in the next second. If you cannot show it, do not say it.

3. Number

Numbers feel specific even when they are small. “4 resume lines US recruiters skip” is a listicle people will watch. “Many resume mistakes” is fog.

4. Mistake

Name the error, not the person. “This is why your rice looks wet on camera” invites a fix. “You are bad at cooking” invites a swipe.

5. POV

POV still works when the scene is boring and true: parking lots, break rooms, apartment hallways. Fantasy POV without a punchline is cosplay.

6. Before / after

Two states. Same kitchen. Same jacket. No miracle body or money claims. If you need a disclaimer, you picked the wrong niche for this site.

7. Loophole

A software or filming workaround — never a legal, tax, or medical loophole. “Album trick for cleaner auto captions” is in bounds.

8. Story open

Start after the boring part. “I almost posted without the overlay” is a story. “So today I wanted to talk about” is a hostage situation.

9. Negative

“Do not say hey guys” is a complete hook if the next frame shows what to say instead.

10. Pattern interrupt

Put a sticky note on the lens. Write the hook on a paper plate. Weird only helps if it is still on-topic.

How to test without lying to yourself

Post two versions of the same video with different first seconds. Keep the rest identical. Watch 1-second hold, not vanity likes. If both flop, the topic is the problem, not the generator.
