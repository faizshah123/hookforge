YouTube Shorts: what to put in the first 3 seconds

Shorts intros fail in three boring ways: a wave, a logo, or a sentence you cannot read. The first three seconds should answer “what is this” without sound.

Second 0–1: picture

Hands already doing the thing. A receipt. A cracked screen. Not your face saying the channel name. Channel name is for the end card.

Second 1–2: overlay

Eight words or fewer, high contrast, away from the like column. Generate overlay-sized lines with the Shorts intro generator.

Second 2–3: voice matches text

If the overlay says “wet rice” and you say “hey y’all welcome back,” you trained the viewer to ignore both.

What not to pack in

  • A 12-word thesis.
  • Three hashtags on screen.
  • A promise you cannot show in 30 seconds.

After the open, you still need a reason to loop. Last frame should make the first frame make sense again. More on spoken vs overlay: hook writing guide.
