CapCut on-screen text tips that do not cover your face

Auto captions are a safety net. They are not a hook. If the first readable words are “um so basically,” you paid for a microphone and used none of it.

Write the overlay before you record

Use the CapCut text generator, copy the on-screen field, paste into CapCut Text. Group words in 2–3 word chunks so they do not wrap into a wall.

Safe area

Leave the right third of the frame for likes and the bottom for captions + TikTok or Shorts UI. Center-left or upper-third is safer than dead center on a talking head.

Contrast beats fonts

A thick sans with a stroke beats a script font that looks “branded” and unreadable on a bus. If you cannot read it at arm’s length, neither can a viewer.

Match the voice

Do not overlay “4 resume lines” while saying “today I want to chat about careers.” Pick one sentence. Film that sentence.
