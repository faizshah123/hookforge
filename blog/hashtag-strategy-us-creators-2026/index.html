Hashtag strategy for US creators in 2026

Hashtags do not rescue a dead first second. They can still help people who search a niche, and they can still hurt you if they are lies.

Rules we actually use in the generator

  • Nine tags or fewer.
  • One niche tag, one format tag, one intent tag.
  • No “fypシ” spam piles.
  • No tags for a city you are not in or a brand you did not use.

Facebook cares less. Instagram still reads the caption. TikTok still pretends it does not care and then surfaces search. Generate per platform on the home tool instead of copying one pack everywhere.

Searchable titles still matter

On-screen text and spoken words that match a real query — “tiny apartment tour,” “Costco lunch” — do more than a wall of tags. Pair this with writing a hook in 10 seconds.
