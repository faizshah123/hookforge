How to use this TikTok hook generator

  1. Pick a niche you actually post. Fake niches make fake hooks.
  2. Write one sentence: what happens on camera, not your life story.
  3. Generate 10 lines. Read them out loud. Keep the one you would say to a friend.
  4. Paste the on-screen text into CapCut. Film the first second on that line, not on “hey guys.”
  5. Use the caption as a draft. Cut anything you would not comment on someone else’s video.

What “good” looks like in 2026

A usable hook is under 12 words, names a specific object or mistake, and does not promise a miracle. “Wait, your rice cooker can do that?” beats “You will not believe this life hack.” Specific beats loud.

We keep health, crypto, and get-rich claims out of the default packs. If your niche is fitness, talk form and filming — not supplements or diagnoses.
