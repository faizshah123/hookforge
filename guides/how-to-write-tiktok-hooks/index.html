How to write TikTok hooks

A hook is the first thing a stranger understands. On TikTok that is usually a spoken line plus on-screen text in the first second. This guide is the manual. The generator is the shortcut.

Step 1 — Pick an object, not a vibe

“Get ready with me” is a vibe. “The concealer I returned to Ulta” is an object. Objects film. Vibes ramble.

Step 2 — One job for the first second

Name the object, the mistake, or the number. Do not name your channel. Do not thank subscribers who are not there yet.

Step 3 — Say it out loud

If you laugh from embarrassment, the line is trying too hard. If you run out of air, it is too long. Under 12 words.

Step 4 — Overlay matches voice

CapCut should show the same sentence, shorter if needed. See on-screen text tips.

Step 5 — Caption does leftover work

Details, a question, hashtags last. Reels especially: caption length.

Step 6 — Test two opens, not two personalities

Same footage, different first second. Read hold time. Then stop tinkering for a week and post.

Worked example

Niche: cooking. Object: wet rice. Question formula: “Wait, your rice looks wet on camera too?” Overlay: “Wet rice. It’s the lid.” Caption: how you vented the cooker, question “lid on or off?”, few tags.
